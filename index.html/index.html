PROBLEM

Solar panels in arid and coastal regions suffer acute efficiency loss from dust accumulation and salt-mist corrosion. Active cleaning systems are energy-intensive and high-maintenance.

APPROACH

Design a passive aerodynamic geometry — inspired by deflector and spoiler principles — that redirects particulate-laden airflow away from the panel surface with zero energy input.

TARGET OUTCOME

A manufacturable shield geometry validated via CFD and physical prototype testing, demonstrating measurable reduction in particle deposition across both environment types.

About

PROFILE.md

I'm a high school student in Algeria with a singular focus: becoming an aerospace systems engineer who builds things that matter. I don't wait for formal education — I learn by doing, failing, and rebuilding.

My approach is rooted in first-principles thinking. I break every problem down to its physical and mathematical fundamentals before reaching for a solution — leading to unconventional ideas that actually work.

I use aggressive time-blocking: dedicated research blocks, hardware lab sessions, and intensive study periods — structured like a research lab schedule, not a student's routine.
